Summon & Hide Mechanics

KyteShelf offers intuitive ways to summon your shelf without interrupting current workflows.

Method 1: Shake to Summon

On your desktop or File Explorer, hold left-click on any file and shake the mouse horizontally 1–2 times. KyteShelf appears instantly beside your cursor. Release the mouse button to drop files in safely.

Method 2: Global Hotkey

Regardless of which full-screen app, browser, or code editor you are in, press the default hotkey Ctrl + ` (the backtick key above Tab) to summon the shelf at your current cursor position!

Smart Pinning & Misclick Protection: Click the "Pin 📌" icon in the top right of the shelf to lock it on top of all windows without auto-hiding.
When unpinned, the shelf gracefully hides whenever you interact with other windows, but all staged items remain 100% intact, ready for the next summon!

Versatile Ingest Modes (Stash Anything)

KyteShelf is not limited to typical files. It connects clipboard snapshots, screenshots, rich text sticky notes, and online image URLs effortlessly.

1. Direct Mouse Drag & Drop (Batch & Outlook Attachments)

Select one or dozens of files and folders, or drag attachments directly from Microsoft Outlook emails into the shelf. KyteShelf automatically extracts file icons, names, and sizes instantly.

2. Clipboard Paste (Ctrl + V)

With the shelf active, press Ctrl + V or click the "Paste" button on the shelf bottom. KyteShelf intelligently interprets the data type:

  • Copied Files from File Explorer ➔ Staged directly into the shelf.
  • Screenshots (e.g. Win + Shift + S or copied images) ➔ Automatically saved as PNG files with live previews!
  • Plain Text & Snippets ➔ Transformed into attractive yellow "Sticky Notes", double-click to expand and edit!
  • Web URLs ➔ Image URLs are fetched and downloaded locally; general links become clickable web bookmarks.
  • Tip: For Microsoft Outlook attachments, dragging directly into the shelf (Mode 1 above) is recommended for seamless compatibility across all Outlook versions.

3. Watch Folder Auto-Ingest

In the tray menu, click "👀 Set Watch Folder..." and pick your "Downloads" or "Desktop" folder. Whenever a new file is downloaded by your browser, KyteShelf intercepts and stages it automatically in the background—no more digging through folders!

Dragging Out, Shelf Clearing & ZIP Bundling

Once you have assembled your collection, transfer items quickly to your target applications or archive them in one step.

📋 Copy Mode vs. 🚚 Move Mode (Auto-Clear on Drop) Click bottom tag to toggle

Click the mode badge at the bottom-left corner of the shelf to toggle between:

  • 📋 Copy Mode (Default): Dragging staged items to applications keeps the shelf items intact, ideal for repetitive pasting.
  • 🚚 Move Mode: Dragging items out into destination windows automatically clears them from the shelf immediately upon successful release.

📦 One-Click ZIP Compression

After gathering multiple documents or photos from scattered folders, click the "ZIP" button at the bottom of the shelf. A clean, standard zip archive is generated locally on your Desktop in a split second!

✉️ Direct Outlook COM Attachment

Select one or multiple items on the shelf, right-click ➔ choose "Attach to Active Outlook Draft". KyteShelf connects directly through native Windows COM APIs to attach files into your open email editor.

KyteView Inter-Process Integration: Space Quick Preview Ecosystem

KyteShelf comes with native Windows Named Pipe IPC integration for KyteView. Select any file on your shelf:

  • Press Space: KyteView pops up instantly with rich content previews (high-res images, 4K videos, audio, PDFs, Office docs, code, and archives).
  • Context Menu: You can also right-click an item and select "👁️ Quick Preview with KyteView".
  • Press Space Again to Toggle: Seamlessly dismiss the preview window at any time.
  • Background Auto-Wake: If KyteView is not running, KyteShelf will automatically wake it up silently in the background; if not installed, it gracefully falls back without any lag.

Preferences & Customization

Right-click the tray icon ➔ select "⚙️ Preferences", or click the gear icon on the shelf toolbar to enter the settings panel. Options are divided into 3 modular sections:

⚡ Summon & Trigger Behaviors

Enable Mouse Shake Summon

Enabled by default. If you prefer to summon the shelf exclusively using hotkeys, uncheck this option to disable mouse shake detection completely.

Shake Sensitivity (Level 1 to 5)

Default is "Level 3 (Standard)". If you move your mouse aggressively, pick Level 1~2 to prevent false triggers; if you prefer micro-shake summoning, choose Level 4~5.

Global Hotkey Recorder

Select from popular presets (like Ctrl + `, Ctrl + Shift + D), or click into the field to record your own custom keys.

Summon Position (Follow Cursor vs Remember Position)

Choose "Follow Mouse Cursor" (shelf appears right next to where you shake), or "Remember Last Window Position" (shelf stays anchored to your favorite screen corner).

📂 Shelf & Drag Behaviors

Launch on Startup

Automatically start KyteShelf minimized in the tray upon Windows login, ensuring your shelf is always ready without manual startup.

Sound Feedback

Plays a subtle, premium feedback click sound whenever files, screenshots, or notes land onto the shelf.

Auto-Remove on Drag Out

In move mode or individual drags, items are automatically cleared from the shelf once dropped onto a target window.

Auto-Hide When Empty

When all items on the shelf are dragged out or cleared, the shelf window gracefully retreats to keep your desktop distraction-free.

Enable KyteView Integration Preview (Space)

Enabled by default. Allows selecting any file in the shelf and pressing Space or using the context menu to summon KyteView for instant rich file previews.

🌐 System & Cache Management

Interface Language

Switch between "System Default", "Traditional Chinese", and "English" on the fly without restarting the application.

Temp Cache Retention Policy

For temporary screenshots and sticky notes created via clipboard, choose "Auto-clean after 7 days" (recommended), "Clear on app exit", or "Never auto-clean".

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Q1: Why doesn't shaking the mouse summon the shelf?

Ensure you are shaking the mouse while holding down the left mouse button on a file. If it still does not respond, open Preferences and confirm "Enable Shake to Summon" is checked, or increase sensitivity to Level 4 or 5.

Q2: Why does Windows Defender or antivirus prompt a warning?

This is standard for low-level desktop utilities. KyteShelf utilizes Windows Global Mouse Hooks to detect file dragging and shaking gestures. KyteShelf runs 100% locally and contains zero telemetry or spyware. Please add it to trusted applications.

Q3: Do I need continuous internet access during daily use?

No! KyteShelf utilizes an offline cryptographic license architecture. Internet connection is required only for one second during key activation. Once validated, offline credentials are cached locally, allowing permanent offline use on planes or disconnected secure environments.

Q4: What is the maintenance period and update policy?
